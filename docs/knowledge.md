# Acme Coffee Roasters – Company Handbook

## About Us
Acme Coffee Roasters was founded in 2015 in Portland, Oregon. We roast small-batch, ethically sourced coffee beans and sell them online and in our two cafés.

## Opening Hours
Our cafés are open Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday and Sunday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We are closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

## Locations
- Downtown Café: 120 Pine Street, Portland, OR 97204
- Riverside Café: 45 Water Avenue, Portland, OR 97214

## Shipping Policy
Orders over $40 ship free within the United States. Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and costs $5.99 for orders under $40. Express shipping (1–2 business days) costs $14.99. We currently do not ship outside the US.

## Return and Refund Policy
Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. If your coffee arrives damaged or you are unhappy with the taste, contact us within 14 days and we will send a replacement or refund you. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Subscriptions
Our Coffee Club subscription delivers fresh beans every 2 or 4 weeks. Subscribers get 10% off every order and free shipping regardless of order size. You can pause, skip, or cancel anytime from your account page.

## Products
- House Blend (medium roast, chocolate and caramel notes) – $14 per 12 oz bag
- Sunrise Espresso (dark roast, bold and smoky) – $16 per 12 oz bag
- Ethiopia Yirgacheffe (light roast, floral and citrus) – $19 per 12 oz bag
- Decaf Swiss Water (medium roast, smooth and nutty) – $15 per 12 oz bag

## Wholesale
Cafés and restaurants can order wholesale with a minimum of 20 lbs per month. Wholesale customers receive 25% off retail prices. Email wholesale@acmecoffee.example to apply.
